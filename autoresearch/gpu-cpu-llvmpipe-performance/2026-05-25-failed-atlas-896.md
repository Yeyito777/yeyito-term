# Failed experiment: 896x896 GPU atlases

## Hypothesis

The accepted 1024x1024 atlases are robust but may be slightly larger than needed
for the llvmpipe benchmark. A moderate 896x896 size could keep most reset avoidance
while reducing texture footprint relative to 1024x1024.

## Patch summary

In `render/gpu.c` `gpuinit()`, the experiment changed:

```c
gpu.atlasw = 1024;
gpu.atlash = 1024;
```

to:

```c
gpu.atlasw = 896;
gpu.atlash = 896;
```

No Xft fallback, `gpudraw` bypass, renderer switch, or llvmpipe detection trick was
used. The actual GPU renderer, text/emoji paths, fractional scaling, clear-color
cache, cleared-background skip, and accepted vimnav row guard stayed active.

## Validation

- `make`
- `make test_gpu_regressions`

Both passed before benchmarking.

## Benchmark

Result:

- `autoresearch/gpu-cpu-llvmpipe-performance/runs/atlas-896/result.json`

## Decision

Rejected and reverted.

The 896 atlas improved emoji wall and matched cursor wall, but repaint regressed
badly and the total score fell well below accepted. Keep the accepted 1024x1024
atlases.
