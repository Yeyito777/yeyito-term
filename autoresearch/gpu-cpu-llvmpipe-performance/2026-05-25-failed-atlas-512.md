# Failed experiment: 512x512 GPU atlases

## Hypothesis

The accepted 1024x1024 glyph and color atlases keep atlas resets low, but they also
increase llvmpipe texture footprint. A smaller 512x512 atlas could improve cache
locality and RSS enough to offset occasional extra resets in the benchmark.

## Patch summary

In `render/gpu.c` `gpuinit()`, the experiment changed:

```c
gpu.atlasw = 1024;
gpu.atlash = 1024;
```

to:

```c
gpu.atlasw = 512;
gpu.atlash = 512;
```

The actual GPU renderer, text/emoji paths, fractional scaling, clear-color cache,
cleared-background skip, and accepted vimnav row guard remained active. No Xft
fallback, `gpudraw` bypass, renderer switch, or llvmpipe detection trick was used.

## Validation

- `make`
- `make test_gpu_regressions`

Both passed before benchmarking.

## Benchmark

Result:

- `autoresearch/gpu-cpu-llvmpipe-performance/runs/atlas-512/result.json`

## Decision

Rejected and reverted.

The smaller atlas helped repaint and ASCII wall ratios and lowered RSS, but cursor,
unicode, and emoji wall ratios remained below accepted, leaving the weighted score
slightly short. Keep the accepted 1024x1024 atlases.
